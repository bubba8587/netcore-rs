## Decision

netcore-rs does what rete and rete-engine do: the same graph changes, the same events in the same order, the same calculated values, the same errors. Where netcore-rs can't or shouldn't match (a case where rete itself breaks, or one JavaScript allows and Rust can't express), the difference and its reason are written down in the port spec, and nowhere else may netcore-rs differ.

## Why

Solenoid already runs on rete, so anything netcore-rs answers differently is a change in Solenoid that nobody chose.
